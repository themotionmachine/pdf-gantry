Started at "a working FTS5+semantic-search CLI... with core composable primitives shipped but the query algebra still shallow," and closed having deepened the *reliability* of that algebra more than its *surface*: no round shipped a wholly new retrieval verb (`--ids-only`, scoped context windows, and cross-paper filtered queries — the Palette's stated horizon — remain unbuilt).
